Durofy is a free AI magazine cover generator that turns any photo into a realistic, newsstand-quality magazine cover in seconds. Upload a photo, choose from 10 art-directed styles, and generate — the first 3 covers are free with no signup needed.

Built as a side project, Durofy is made for creators, marketers, and anyone who wants a fun, shareable way to put themselves on a magazine cover. Each cover is generated with art-directed prompts for realistic lighting, typography, and layout.
